# Honest-limits language (use verbatim or close to it)

On binding evidence:
- "Every number in sections 1–5 is a structure predictor's opinion. The predictors used here and in design were trained on the same public structure database, so their agreement is correlated evidence, not independent replication."
- "The controls show the pipeline is not trivially fooled — it separates a known binder from scrambled and decoy sequences — not that these designs bind."
- "Published calibration says confidence filters of this kind raise wet-lab hit rates (roughly an order of magnitude over physics-only filtering) and do not predict affinity or rank binders by strength."
- "A design that is confidently predicted in a pose other than the designed one has failed this check even if its confidence is high."
- If a consensus rule was used to select designs: "Because this rule was used for selection, it is part of the objective for the final set and no longer an independent check."

On developability:
- "There is no validated computational developability gauntlet for de novo miniproteins; antibody flag sets (TAP, clinical-stage assay panels) are calibrated on IgGs and do not transfer. Values are shown against reference proteins for orientation, not as pass/fail."
- "Solubility, expression, aggregation, stability and protease resistance are measured properties; the descriptors here flag risks and cannot clear them."
- "Pharmacokinetics is determined mainly by size and format at this scale, not by sequence; no PK is predicted here beyond the format note."
- "Immunogenicity risk has a computable component (predicted T-cell epitope content, aggregation) and non-computable ones (impurities, route, target biology, pre-existing antibodies); the decisive assessments are in vitro and clinical."

On scope:
- "Not checked (section 9) lists everything this dossier does not cover; absence from the flag lists above is not evidence of absence."
